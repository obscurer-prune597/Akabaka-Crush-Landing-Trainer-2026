## 🎮 About the Game

Sucker for Love: Crush Landing is a horror-comedy romance visual novel and point-and-click puzzle adventure developed by Akabaka and released on August 31, 2026. The story follows a sentient comet named Hheily that crash-lands in the protagonist's apartment, creating a colorful Lovecraftian crisis filled with choices, visitors, puzzles, and multiple endings.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sucker for Love: Crush Landing**. This trainer enhances the game's apartment exploration and resource-management mechanics. It can pause the countdown, restore collectible offerings, preserve item colors, and protect the current route from common resource mistakes while leaving dialogue choices and story progression under the player's control.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore every apartment interaction, test different dialogue choices, reach multiple endings, or reduce the pressure of the timed flame-feeding and color-management puzzles.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Flame Countdown** — Stops the active countdown while you explore the apartment, inspect objects, and plan the next offering.
- **Restore Colored Offerings** — Restores consumed color-coded items so you can continue solving the flame-feeding puzzle without restarting.
- **Preserve Item Colors** — Prevents colored apartment objects from being permanently desaturated after Hheily consumes an offering.
- **Unlimited Interaction Attempts** — Allows repeated examination, pickup, and use attempts during apartment and hallway exploration.
- **Visitor Delay** — Delays timed visitor events at the apartment door while you manage the flame and investigate available items.
- **Safe Route Protection** — Protects the active session from common resource-lock situations caused by using important offerings in an unhelpful order.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat Mode** — Crush Landing is primarily a visual novel and puzzle adventure rather than a conventional combat game, so the trainer provides no weapon or damage enhancements.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the fully navigable hand-drawn apartment and nearby hallway by examining rooms, counters, appliances, plants, doors, and other interactive objects. Use Freeze Flame Countdown before leaving Hheily's area when you need time to search for a suitable offering.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect and inspect colored objects from the apartment, including food, gifts, plants, and other usable items. Because feeding Hheily can affect other objects of the same color, use Restore Colored Offerings or Preserve Item Colors when experimenting with different item combinations.

### World & Environment

The apartment is the central puzzle space. Investigate environmental changes caused by Hheily, answer or refuse visitors at the door, and use hallway interactions to uncover dialogue, lore, and route opportunities. Trainer options should be activated after the room has finished loading.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no conventional combat system with weapons, attacks, or enemy health bars. Threats are handled through exploration, timed decisions, dialogue choices, item management, and story events. The trainer therefore focuses on puzzle resources and timing rather than combat statistics.
